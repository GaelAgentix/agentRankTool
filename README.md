# Automation Candidate Ranking

A visual aid for Ideal Execution interviews. Share your screen and drag the client's automation candidates into order. The top three are highlighted.

**Open it: https://gaelagentix.github.io/agentRankTool/**

## How to use

1. Paste your candidates, one per line, as `Name - short summary`, then click **Save list**.
2. Drag rows to rank them.
3. Click the X to rule a candidate out. The arrow brings it back.
4. Double-click a name or summary to reword it.
5. Click **Copy results** to copy the ranking for your notes.

**Edit list** reopens the text box to add, remove or replace candidates.

## Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. The list stays in your browser tab: it survives a refresh, and closing the tab clears it. Copy your results before you close the tab.

## Changing it

Everything is in `index.html`. It follows the Agentix Live house style (`ashahid-agentix/fde-house-style`). Changes merged to `main` go live on the link above automatically.
